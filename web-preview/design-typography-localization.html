Typography & Localization Stress Lab

The visual contract must survive translation expansion, CJK glyph density, RTL direction and large-text settings without weakening decision hierarchy.

Long English
The northern delegates request an immediate audience before the council closes for the winter session.
German expansion
Die nördlichen Gesandten verlangen eine sofortige Audienz, bevor der Rat seine Wintersitzung beendet.
CJK probe
北方の使節団は、冬季会議が終わる前に直ちに謁見することを求めています。
RTL probe
يطلب مبعوثو الشمال مقابلة فورية قبل انتهاء جلسة المجلس الشتوية.
Typography rules

Use semantic hierarchy, not fixed line counts.

Allow wrapping before truncation.

Keep decision controls visible at large text.

Do not assume left-to-right punctuation or icon meaning.

Release gate

Every supported locale needs representative long-string review on each critical screen. This lab validates design resilience; translation quality and Android font rendering remain separate verification tasks.
