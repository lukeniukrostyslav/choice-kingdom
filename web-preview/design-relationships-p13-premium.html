P13 · Relationships / character state

Make every relationship feel remembered.

The relationship surface turns past choices into a readable human state: who matters, what changed, why the player believes it, and what may change next. It avoids binary “good/bad” meters and never hides meaning behind color.

Production contract: relationship state is derived from canonical gameplay/evidence. This surface is a visual contract and uses no invented production character IDs or gameplay rules.
