# Choice Kingdom — P2 Character Art System v1

Status: **P2 STARTED — SYSTEM FOUNDATION**

## Goal

Build a repeatable premium character-art system before expanding the People/Character catalog. P2 is not a character-count exercise; it defines how characters remain recognizable, attractive, coherent and readable across mobile surfaces.

## Character hierarchy

### Tier A — major recurring characters

- strongest facial identity;
- highest portrait finish;
- richer costume/material treatment;
- multiple emotional states;
- highest crop and lighting consistency requirements.

### Tier B — recurring supporting characters

- clear silhouette and face identity;
- controlled costume variation;
- fewer high-detail variants;
- consistent faction/context cues.

### Tier C — secondary characters

- strong role/silhouette read;
- simpler material/detail budget;
- reusable pose families where appropriate.

## Identity anchors

Every recurring character receives:

1. identity ID;
2. age band;
3. silhouette/proportion family;
4. face landmarks;
5. hair/headwear anchor;
6. costume anchor;
7. faction/context cues;
8. posture language;
9. expression range;
10. lighting/framing profile;
11. crop-safe region;
12. provenance/licensing record.

## Portrait composition

Primary portrait is portrait-mobile first.

- Eyes and facial landmarks stay inside the protected region.
- Head and shoulders must survive thumbnail reduction.
- Hands/props are secondary narrative anchors.
- Background detail is quieter than the face.
- Costume silhouette must remain identifiable without reading metadata.

## Expression system

The base expression family is:

- neutral / observant;
- concerned;
- guarded;
- relieved;
- angry;
- calculating;
- vulnerable;
- resolute.

Expressions must preserve character identity rather than becoming interchangeable emotional masks.

## Lighting system

Character families use one dominant light direction with controlled fill. Major characters may receive restrained rim separation, but lighting must remain compatible with Event, People and Ending surfaces.

## Costume system

Costume communicates:

- social position;
- profession/function;
- faction/context;
- wealth/material access;
- current narrative condition.

Variation may alter wear, damage, season, ceremonial state or context without breaking identity.

## Mobile readability gate

A character fails P2 review if, at target mobile size:

- silhouette becomes ambiguous;
- face loses identity;
- costume turns into noise;
- expression cannot be distinguished;
- crop removes the identity anchor;
- background competes with the face.

## Anti-drift rules

Do not introduce:

- generic stock fantasy faces;
- interchangeable body proportions;
- arbitrary lighting between portraits;
- faction color-coding that implies morality;
- excessive facial retouching that erases age/history;
- glossy gacha-style presentation inconsistent with Avelune;
- decorative effects that hide expression or silhouette.

## P2 acceptance gate

P2 can reach 100% only when:

- character construction system is represented in inspectable assets;
- primary and secondary anchor characters are stable;
- expression and pose families are defined;
- portrait crop rules are verified at 360dp and 412dp;
- light/dark and RTL implications are checked;
- provenance/licensing fields exist for final assets;
- visual drift review passes;
- evidence is committed to GitHub.
