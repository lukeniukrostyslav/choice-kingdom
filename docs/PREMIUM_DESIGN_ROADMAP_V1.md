# Choice Kingdom — Premium Design Roadmap v1

Status: **APPROVED**

This roadmap is the post-V1–V18 visual-polish phase. V1–V18 remain closed at their defined design-specification boundaries. This roadmap exists to raise the actual product presentation from a completed design specification/prototype toward premium commercial-game visual quality.

## Quality target
Choice Kingdom should present as a premium narrative mobile game: cohesive art direction, memorable characters, strong visual hierarchy, cinematic decision moments, restrained motion, excellent mobile readability, and production-quality assets. The goal is quality, not merely completion of checklists.

## Competitive-quality principles

- Establish a strict, memorable visual language before scaling assets.
- Use consistent character framing, lighting, proportions and identity cues.
- Prioritize the decision surface and narrative stakes over decorative UI.
- Use restrained motion for feedback and atmosphere rather than spectacle.
- Treat mobile readability, touch targets, safe areas, long strings and RTL as part of visual quality.
- Allocate visual detail by importance: key characters, event illustrations, world/realm moments and endings receive the highest treatment.
- Final assets require stable identity, crop-safe composition, provenance/licensing and QA.
