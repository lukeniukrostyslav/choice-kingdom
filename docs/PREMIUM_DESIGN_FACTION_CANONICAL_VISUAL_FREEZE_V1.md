## Purpose

This file freezes what may and may not be treated as canonical in the premium faction visual layer.

The previously generated six-name faction poster is **visual concept material only**. Its labels, mottos, locations, heraldry and relationship examples are not promoted to game canon unless independently present in authoritative source data.

The current authoritative QA vocabulary explicitly distinguishes these positions/voices:

1. **Crown**
2. **Commons**
3. **Noble**
4. **Guild**
5. **Border / Security**
6. **Civic / Medical**

Evidence: `docs/CONTENT_QA_MATRIX.md` under “Faction integrity”.

These are **institutional/factional positions used by the narrative**, not six confirmed named kingdoms with canonical heraldry.

## P25 visual rule

Faction visuals must use the verified narrative vocabulary first. No new faction name, ID, crest, motto, territory or relationship state may be invented to fill an art sheet.

For the final premium visual treatment:

- use distinct geometric/material grammars for institutional forces;
- do not encode good/evil through color;
- preserve deep blue-black / charcoal foundation and restrained warm-gold accents;
- use refined medieval-fantasy materials: aged metal, stone, wood, woven cloth, leather, wax/seal and parchment;
- keep labels semantically neutral;
- support compact phone, tablet and expanded layouts;
- preserve RTL, large text, long strings, safe areas and reduced motion;
- relationship states must come from game state, never from artwork;
- final heraldry is deferred until a canonical faction inventory/ID binding is established.

## Character follow-on

After this faction visual freeze, the same verification procedure applies to the character layer:

- canonical People names/IDs must be verified from source;
- visual anchors may be frozen;
- invented personality, motive, relationship state, faction membership or availability must not be written into canon;
- the six verified recurring People currently present in the authoritative event catalog/source patch are Mara, Rowan, Seris, Ivo, Amara and Toma;
- final production portraits still require canonical binding/provenance/production approval before being called fully canonical.
