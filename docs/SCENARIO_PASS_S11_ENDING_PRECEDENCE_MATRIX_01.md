# Choice Kingdom — S11 Ending Precedence Matrix 01

Status: ACTIVE — SCENARIO ONLY
Scope: E251–E272

## Gate

S11 is not closed by having ending prose. Every ending family must have explicit positive prerequisites, explicit negative blockers, deterministic precedence, and a fresh-run path.

## Ending families

- Steward
- Iron Crown
- Golden Compact
- People's Charter
- Broken Diadem
- Quiet Throne
- Second Founder

## Required checks

1. Every positive prerequisite has an authoritative upstream producer.
2. No ending family can manufacture its own prerequisite.
3. Support evidence is not silently promoted to a final predicate.
4. Negative blockers are evaluated independently from positive evidence.
5. Mutually qualifying endings have deterministic precedence.
6. Replay/meta-only evidence is not substituted for same-run state.
7. Every intended ending has at least one fresh-run reachable qualification route.
8. Terminal selection is deterministic for identical state.

## Known high-risk boundaries

- `pred.final_charter_prerequisites`: E209 is consumer-only and cannot serve as its own producer.
- `pred.coalition_cooperation`: E261 `four_way_bargain` is support evidence only; authoritative cooperation qualification remains required.
- Second Founder: requires systemic explanation, cross-faction cooperation, institutional redesign, and replay-sensitive qualification without circular promotion.

## Closure rule

S11 remains at 60% until the authoritative prerequisite catalog, precedence ordering, and fresh-run reachability checks all pass. Writing or auditing this matrix alone does not increase the percentage.
