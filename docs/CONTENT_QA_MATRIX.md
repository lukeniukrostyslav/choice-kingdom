This document is the narrative production gate. It exists so content progress is measured by causal integrity, not by the number of written cards.

## Required narrative gates

### Causal depth
- [ ] Every major event has a downstream consequence.
- [ ] At least 10 consequences are delayed by 3+ turns.
- [ ] At least 6 branch locks are intentional and documented.
- [ ] At least 6 late events explicitly callback earlier decisions.
- [ ] At least 4 investigation decisions change what information is available or how later evidence is interpreted.
- [ ] At least 4 choices that are costly when made become useful later.
- [ ] No critical ending requires liking one particular character.

### Character integrity
- [ ] Mara has a positive institutional route and a negative/resignation route.
- [ ] Rowan has a lawful-defense route and a militarist/patronage route.
- [ ] Seris has a reform/constitutional route and an old-order route.
- [ ] Ivo has a public-market route and a commercial-leverage route.
- [ ] Amara has a civic-relief route and a centralization conflict.
- [ ] Toma has a trusted-information route and a rejected/less-reliable-information route.

### Faction integrity
- [ ] Crown position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Commons position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Noble position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Guild position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Border/security position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] No faction is reduced to a permanent villain role.

### Investigation integrity
- [ ] Mara route can expose accounting structure.
- [ ] Toma route can expose physical movement of evidence.
- [ ] Seris route can expose elite participation.
- [ ] Direct decree/account route can prove document fraud.
- [ ] At least two independent evidence routes can reach the systemic explanation.
- [ ] The final mystery does not require a single mastermind reveal.

### Replay integrity
- [ ] Second playthrough can reveal information hidden in the first.
- [ ] Different early choices produce different mid-game options.
- [ ] At least one ending has two independent prerequisite routes.
- [ ] At least one ending becomes easier through information rather than raw stats.
- [ ] Replay differences are causal, not random text shuffling.

## Content expansion gate

The current E01–E70 set is the authored spine, not the final volume. Before engine freeze:

1. Expand the campaign to approximately 250–350+ meaningful authored events/story nodes.
2. Insert character, faction, ordinary-life, economic, security, investigation, callback and replay-exclusive nodes around the spine.
3. Run contradiction and reachability analysis.
4. Check that every node has a downstream reason to exist.
5. Remove filler/repetitive cards.
6. Balance turn frequency and pressure escalation.
7. Verify all ending paths through deterministic scenario simulations once the engine exists.

## Definition of content-ready

Content is **CONTENT READY** only when the full catalog is authored, causally linked, internally consistent, ending-reachable, replay-differentiated, localized-key-complete, and reviewed against this matrix. A large number of written cards without these properties does not count as completion.
