## Required narrative gates

### Causal depth
- [ ] Every major event has a downstream consequence.
- [ ] At least 10 consequences delayed 3+ turns.
- [ ] At least 6 intentional mutually exclusive branch locks.
- [ ] At least 6 late callback events explicitly reference earlier history.
- [ ] At least 4 investigation decisions change available information or interpretation.
- [ ] At least 4 earlier costly choices become useful later.
- [ ] No critical ending requires liking one particular character.

### Character integrity
- [ ] Mara: positive institutional route and resignation/negative route.
- [ ] Rowan: lawful-defense route and militarist/patronage route.
- [ ] Seris: reform route and old-order route.
- [ ] Ivo: public-market route and commercial-leverage route.
- [ ] Amara: civic-relief route and centralization conflict.
- [ ] Toma: trusted-information route and rejected/less-reliable route.

### Faction integrity
- [ ] Crown position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Commons position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Noble position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Guild position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] Border/security position is sometimes reasonable.
- [ ] No faction is a permanent villain.

### Investigation integrity
- [ ] Mara route exposes accounting structure.
- [ ] Toma route exposes physical evidence movement.
- [ ] Seris route exposes elite participation.
- [ ] Direct decree/account route proves document fraud.
- [ ] At least two independent routes reach the systemic explanation.
- [ ] A single mastermind is not required.

### Replay integrity
- [ ] Second playthrough can reveal information hidden in the first.
- [ ] Different early choices produce different mid-game options.
- [ ] At least one ending has two independent prerequisite routes.
- [ ] At least one ending becomes easier through information rather than raw stats.
- [ ] Replay differences are causal, not shuffled text.

## Definition of content-ready
