## Full-catalog QA tasks

1. Build one canonical index for E01–E270; preserve stable IDs and source provenance.
2. For every node, record prerequisites, turn window, state/relationship effects, flags, delayed effects, follow-up candidates and ending relevance.
3. Detect duplicate IDs, duplicate flags, contradictory flag meanings and incompatible resource assumptions.
4. Detect nodes that have no incoming route, no outgoing consequence, or only a cosmetic outgoing consequence.
5. Detect branch locks that permanently remove all viable endings.
6. Detect branches that differ only numerically or textually rather than causally.
7. Detect delayed effects that can resolve twice or never resolve.
8. Detect events whose trigger depends on a flag that no authored choice can set.
9. Detect events whose required flag is removed before the event can resolve.
10. Detect mutually exclusive branches that accidentally rejoin without acknowledging the divergence.
11. Detect ending prerequisites that depend on one character alone.
12. Detect pacing clusters where too many high-impact events fire in the same turn window.
13. Detect resource combinations that make a meaningful choice mathematically impossible too often.
14. Verify every ending has a recognizable causal route and at least two independent prerequisite configurations where the design calls for it.
15. Remove or rewrite filler nodes discovered during the audit.

## Definition of content-ready
