# Choice Kingdom — Block 16 Ending / Resolution Design Closure

## Premium contract
- Ending is an authored emotional landing, not a generic win/lose score screen.
- The chronicle distinguishes what changed, what endured, and what remains unresolved.
- Previous decisions and remembered relationships are surfaced as narrative continuity.
- The player can review the full chronicle, begin another reign, or return to the kingdom without losing the ending record.
- No single numerical rating replaces the authored consequence story.
- Responsive, RTL, large-text and reduced-motion behavior preserve the same hierarchy.
- Safe-area-aware mobile presentation is supported.
