# Choice Kingdom — Ending Producer Gap Register 01

Date: 2026-09-15  
Status: **SOURCE-LEVEL QA — OPEN**  
Scope: seven ending families

## Purpose

Separate ending qualification design from actual authored producer closure. A named prerequisite is not considered implemented merely because an ending contract describes it.

| Ending | Critical prerequisite families | Producer closure | Main remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steward | institutional reform; restrained/expired emergency power; legitimacy; no collapse | **PARTIAL** | exact canonical producers for the institutional thread, emergency-power restraint and terminal blockers |
| Iron Crown | border/military route; concentrated authority; retained emergency authority / weak limits | **PARTIAL** | exact machine-readable qualification and negative constitutional blockers |
| Golden Compact | commercial thread; durable guild/economic leverage; economic stability | **PARTIAL** | exact `pred.guild_influence_strong`, economic stability predicate and commercial thread producer set |
| People's Charter | civic legitimacy; institutional/civic route; durable public participation; no authoritarian/collapse blocker | **PARTIAL** | exact civic participation predicate and blocker producer closure |
| Broken Diadem | deterministic terminal failure predicate | **OPEN** | failure predicate and at least two independent authored failure paths are not frozen |
| Quiet Throne | explicit withdrawal/abdication/low-intervention history | **OPEN** | exact authored withdrawal producer and precedence against positive endings |
| Second Founder | archive/investigation; `pred.systemic_explanation_verified`; `pred.coalition_cooperation`; constitutional redesign; constrained/expired emergency power | **PARTIAL** | systemic-evidence convergence, coalition predicate, constitutional prerequisite and replay-meta producers |

## Cross-ending blockers still requiring source closure

1. Terminal collapse must be a real predicate, not a low-score shortcut.
2. Quiet Throne must require explicit authored withdrawal history.
3. Positive-ending precedence must be authored as data.
4. `pred.systemic_explanation_verified` needs distinct evidence identities and a convergence rule.
5. `pred.coalition_cooperation` cannot be inferred from `four_way_bargain` alone.
6. Replay metadata must not be substituted with ordinary history or flags.

## Current conclusion

The ending contracts are sufficiently specified to guide further producer audits, but they are not ready for runtime fixtures. This register intentionally prevents the ending resolver from being implemented against prose-only prerequisites.
