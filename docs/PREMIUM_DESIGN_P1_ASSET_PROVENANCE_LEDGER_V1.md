## Required record for every final asset

Before an asset is treated as release-final, record:
- creator / commissioning source;
- creation or delivery date;
- original source/reference where applicable;
- ownership or license basis;
- permitted commercial use;
- modification/derivative rights where applicable;
- attribution requirement where applicable;
- stable asset ID and final repository SHA;
- approval/QA owner.

## Important boundary

These SVGs are valid repository/rendering evidence for the visual language, but this ledger deliberately does **not** claim that they are licensed final production artwork. P1 therefore remains open until provenance evidence is supplied and the rendered/runtime exit gate passes.
