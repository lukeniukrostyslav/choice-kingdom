# Choice Kingdom — Block 15 Crisis / High-Stakes Design Closure

## Scope
P15 — Crisis / High-Stakes Presentation. The visual goal is cinematic urgency without clutter, panic-color dependence, or misleading certainty.

## Premium visual contract
- Crisis identity is established through composition, hierarchy, typography and restrained material contrast.
- The decision window is explicit.
- Known, pending and uncertain consequences are separated.
- Available orders remain readable under pressure.
- Consequence previews communicate direction/timing without pretending the future is fully known.
- Risk is not reduced to a binary good/bad color signal.
- The same semantic hierarchy survives responsive layouts, RTL and large text.
- Reduced motion removes decorative movement without hiding state or consequence information.
- Safe-area-aware presentation remains compatible with mobile surfaces.
