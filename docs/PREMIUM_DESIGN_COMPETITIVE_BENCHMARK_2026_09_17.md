# Choice Kingdom — Premium Design Competitive Benchmark 2026-09-17

## Purpose

Use current premium mobile-game design research as a quality benchmark without copying competitor art, characters, layouts, branding, or proprietary assets.

## Research signals

- Monument Valley demonstrates an art-led experience where each screen is treated as a composed visual experience; its design philosophy emphasizes elegance and interaction that can be learned through doing. (GDC Vault; GameDeveloper; GameDesign.gg)
- Premium narrative/mobile references continue to emphasize restrained UI, touch-native interaction, atmosphere, and complete experiences rather than F2P chrome. (current 2026 premium-mobile reviews)
- Current mobile UX guidance emphasizes thumb reach, safe areas, responsive feedback, progressive disclosure, accessibility, and designing on an actual phone rather than scaling down desktop layouts.
- Contemporary game-UI production guidance treats hierarchy, feedback, accessibility and responsive behavior as production disciplines rather than cosmetic polish.

## Choice Kingdom design consequences

1. **Art leads; chrome recedes.** The narrative image, situation and human stakes own the visual hierarchy.
2. **One dominant action.** The decision surface must be visually obvious without looking like a generic form.
3. **Touch is narrative.** Selection, resolving and consequence transitions need meaningful visual rhythm instead of arbitrary animation.
4. **Editorial composition.** Screens should feel composed like illustrated pages: deliberate whitespace, controlled type scale and restrained ornament.
5. **Material restraint.** Avoid glossy F2P gradients, excessive gold, noisy particles and decorative UI that competes with the story.
6. **Human specificity.** Characters and relationships must read as authored people, not interchangeable portrait cards.
7. **State clarity without spoilers.** Feedback can communicate interaction state while hidden future consequences remain hidden.
8. **Mobile-native geometry.** Safe areas, thumb reach, large targets, portrait composition and short visual scanning distances are first-class constraints.
9. **Accessible luxury.** Focus visibility, contrast, large text, reduced motion and RTL must preserve the premium composition rather than appear as afterthoughts.
10. **Every surface earns its ornament.** If removing a decorative element improves hierarchy or comprehension, remove it.

## Current production application

P6 Choice Experience is the active block. Its visual benchmark now includes real authored choice-family coverage and a premium choice-chamber prototype. The next closure gate is rendered verification of the family surfaces and explicit handoff into P7 Consequence Experience.

## Anti-copy rule

Competitors are used only as quality references and UX/design evidence. No competitor artwork, characters, logos, screenshots, proprietary visual assets, or distinctive layouts are copied into Choice Kingdom.
