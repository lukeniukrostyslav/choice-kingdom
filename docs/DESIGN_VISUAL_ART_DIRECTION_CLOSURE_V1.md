# Choice Kingdom — Visual Language + Art Direction Closure v1

Status: CLOSED DESIGN CONTRACT

## Visual language

Choice Kingdom uses a restrained chronicle/kingdom visual language:

- parchment-like warm canvas semantics;
- dark ink hierarchy for narrative readability;
- restrained authority blue, warning amber, stability green and chronicle gold accents;
- cards and decision controls use consistent rounded geometry;
- borders and elevation distinguish hierarchy without relying on shadows alone;
- imagery supports narrative context and never hides or replaces the decision itself.

## Art direction rules

- Characters are represented as people/roles, not generic morality icons.
- Factions use identity marks and stance states rather than simplistic good/evil color coding.
- Ending art communicates narrative closure and atmosphere, not a gameplay shortcut.
- Investigation visuals prioritize evidence relationships over decoration.
- Asset crops must preserve the focal subject at all supported aspect ratios.
- Missing/fallback assets must preserve layout geometry and never look like a broken decision control.

## Consistency rules

All visual assets inherit the semantic palette, typography hierarchy, spacing scale, corner radius and accessibility rules from `DESIGN_TOKENS_V1.json`.
