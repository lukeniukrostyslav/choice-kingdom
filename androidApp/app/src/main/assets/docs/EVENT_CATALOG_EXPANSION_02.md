# Choice Kingdom — Campaign Expansion II (E71–E110)

These nodes expand the campaign between the authored spine and final endings. They are not filler: each creates information, relationship, faction, resource, access, or future-state consequences.

## Act II/III expansion — lives behind the ledger

### E71 — The Baker's Scale
A baker proves that official grain prices do not match what families actually pay.
- A inspect weights: +trust, unlocks market evidence.
- B subsidize bread: -gold, +trust, creates later treasury pressure.

### E72 — Three Missing Barrels
Customs records show three barrels vanished without a recorded owner.
- A trace manifests: unlocks Toma route.
- B replace the loss quietly: +power, later audit contradiction.

### E73 — The Widow's Petition
A soldier's widow asks for a pension promised by an old decree.
- A honor it: -gold, +trust, strengthens Rowan's lawful route.
- B renegotiate: +power, -trust, creates military resentment.

### E74 — The Guild Apprentice
A young apprentice says the guild is forcing merchants to use one supplier.
- A protect the apprentice: +trust, -Ivo.
- B hear guild leaders first: +Ivo, unlocks commercial reform route.

### E75 — The Quiet Archive
Mara finds duplicate copies of emergency orders.
- A catalogue them: unlocks institutional evidence.
- B destroy duplicates: +power, closes one future evidence route.

### E76 — Rain on the Border Road
Refugees arrive after a disputed patrol incident.
- A admit them temporarily: -gold, +trust, increases border pressure.
- B close the road: +security, -reputation.

### E77 — The House Physician
A noble physician admits that elite exemptions are causing medicine shortages.
- A end exemptions: +trust, -Seris.
- B create a private levy: +gold, +Seris, lower public trust.

### E78 — The Public Ledger Day
Citizens gather to read tax records.
- A publish everything: +trust, -power.
- B publish selected totals: +power, leaves suspicion alive.

### E79 — A Favor Returned
Ivo offers to waive a guild fee if the Crown approves another charter.
- A refuse exchange: +trust, -Ivo.
- B accept: +gold, commercial leverage increases.

### E80 — Rowan's Patrol Map
Rowan presents two maps: one optimized for defense, one for civilian safety.
- A military priority: +security, -trust.
- B civilian corridors: +trust, slight security cost.

## Investigation branch

### E81 — Ink That Should Not Be There
A ledger page uses ink unavailable in the palace archive.
- A trace supplier: unlocks procurement route.
- B preserve page untouched: stronger evidence later.

### E82 — The Night Clerk
A frightened clerk knows who changed an emergency account.
- A protect the clerk: +trust, unlocks testimony.
- B promise protection but publish name: +power, weaker witness route.

### E83 — The False Date
A decree is dated before the official crisis that supposedly caused it.
- A expose chronology: +power, unlocks systemic explanation.
- B conceal discrepancy: short-term stability, later credibility loss.

### E84 — Toma's Route
Toma identifies a route used by couriers who never appear in palace records.
- A follow openly: +security, risks route disappearing.
- B follow secretly: -security now, stronger evidence later.

### E85 — Seris's Family Register
An old family register reveals who benefited from emergency appointments.
- A publish names: +trust, -Seris.
- B redact minors and bystanders: +Seris, preserves reform alliance.

### E86 — The Auditor's Notebook
The missing auditor's notebook is found in a chapel.
- A publish all: +trust, implicates allies.
- B verify entries first: -power, stronger evidence.

### E87 — The Chapel Door
Someone tried to burn the notebook but stopped halfway.
- A investigate the fire: unlocks sabotage route.
- B protect the chapel first: +Amara, delays evidence.

### E88 — The Cooper's Invoice
A craftsman proves emergency purchases were inflated.
- A compensate him: -gold, +trust.
- B use him as confidential witness: +power, later testimony risk.

### E89 — The Name Repeated
Three unrelated records contain the same office name.
- A investigate the office: unlocks network branch.
- B hunt the named official: creates false-mastermind risk.

### E90 — No Mastermind
Evidence contradicts the idea of one mastermind.
- A accept systemic corruption: unlocks Second Founder support.
- B continue the hunt: unlocks aggressive investigation route but harms coalition trust.

## Winter pressure expansion

### E91 — Empty Schoolhouse
A schoolhouse becomes a shelter during winter.
- A fund it: -gold, +trust.
- B requisition it for soldiers: +security, -trust.

### E92 — Frozen River
Trade stops for a week.
- A open emergency crossings: +gold later, security risk.
- B restrict crossings: +security, -gold.

### E93 — The Coal Ledger
Heating fuel disappears from municipal stores.
- A audit stores: +Mara, delays relief.
- B buy replacement fuel: -gold, +trust.

### E94 — The Captain's Doubt
Rowan privately questions whether emergency authority is becoming permanent.
- A reassure him with limits: +Rowan, strengthens lawful route.
- B demand loyalty: +security, strengthens militarist route.

### E95 — The Noble Soup Kitchen
Seris proposes nobles fund a public kitchen for political legitimacy.
- A accept: +trust, +Seris.
- B expose the political motive: +trust, -Seris.

### E96 — The Guild Stores
Ivo offers warehouse space for emergency food.
- A accept without conditions: +trust now, future guild leverage.
- B require public accounting: +trust, -Ivo, strengthens oversight.

### E97 — The Lantern Caravan
Amara organizes traveling healers.
- A protect the caravan: -security, +trust.
- B attach soldiers: +security, -Amara.

### E98 — Rumor of a Coup
Toma hears officers discussing a possible seizure of the palace.
- A investigate quietly: unlocks military conspiracy branch.
- B announce the rumor: +trust among citizens, risks panic.

### E99 — The Empty Barracks
An officer claims the rumored coup is imaginary, but a barracks ledger is missing.
- A search: +security, risks military resentment.
- B trust the officer: +Rowan, later evidence may surface.

### E100 — The Winter Vote
Council members demand emergency authority be renewed.
- A refuse: +trust, -power, activates constitutional pressure.
- B renew temporarily: +power, -trust, strengthens Iron Crown path.

## Constitutional expansion

### E101 — The Local Council
Villages ask for authority over relief spending.
- A devolve funds: +trust, -power.
- B retain central control: +power, administrative efficiency.

### E102 — The Public Account
A citizen asks why Crown expenditures are secret.
- A open accounts: +trust, -reputation among elites.
- B protect state secrets: +power, suspicion grows.

### E103 — The Noble Minority
Seris proposes guaranteed minority protections for old houses.
- A accept protections without hereditary seats: +Seris, +trust.
- B reject all special guarantees: +trust, -Seris.

### E104 — The Merchant Minority
Ivo proposes a commercial court independent of the Crown.
- A accept: +Ivo, faster commerce, guild influence.
- B keep ordinary courts: +trust, -Ivo.

### E105 — The Soldier's Clause
Rowan asks for explicit rules governing military orders.
- A require written lawful orders: +trust, -security flexibility.
- B preserve emergency discretion: +security, -trust.

### E106 — The Relief Clause
Amara proposes a constitutional minimum for famine relief.
- A guarantee it: -gold, +trust.
- B leave it to annual budgets: +power, fiscal flexibility.

### E107 — The Information Clause
Toma argues that citizens need protected channels for reporting corruption.
- A create protected reporting: +trust, +Toma.
- B keep reports within government: +power, weaker public oversight.

### E108 — The Three Drafts
Mara presents three constitutional drafts: central, balanced, and distributed.
- A central draft: +power.
- B balanced draft: +trust +institutional stability.
- C distributed draft: +trust -power, stronger civic route.

### E109 — The Compromise That Costs
Every faction can accept a compromise, but each must surrender something.
- A force a single compromise: +power, one faction becomes resentful.
- B let factions trade concessions: -power, +trust, unlocks coalition route.

### E110 — The Bell Before Dawn
The constitutional vote begins as the city waits outside the hall.
- A invite citizens to witness: +trust, public accountability.
- B close the doors: +power, elite bargaining, reduced legitimacy.
